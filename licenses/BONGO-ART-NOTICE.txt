Bongo Cat artwork and attribution
=================================

This Rainy75 V2 package adapts the Bongo Cat keyboard scene for a 320 × 172 display.

Original cat artwork: StrayRogue.
Bongo Cat meme: DitzyFlama.
Reference and credits: https://github.com/Externalizable/bongo.cat

Cat and paw layers: Bongo Cat Mver 0.1.6 F1–F12 skin.
Upstream project: https://github.com/MMmmmoko/Bongo-Cat-Mver

Desk edge and arrow-key artwork: ayangweb/BongoCat, Copyright 2025-present ayangweb.
Source: https://github.com/ayangweb/BongoCat/blob/47a73ed1765b13fdd674ec356d51b5b16a6b5a5b/resources/models/keyboard/resources/background.png
Upstream Apache-2.0 project license: ayangweb-BongoCat-Apache-2.0.txt

This adaptation crops and rescales the artwork, draws the slanted keyboard,
maps the native key notifications to cap highlights and paw poses, and
encodes the resulting frame and sprites in the GIF and screen firmware.

The project’s own source code is MIT-licensed. Third-party artwork remains
credited to its respective rights holders.
